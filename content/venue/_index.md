## Getting to Bonn

**By train:** Bonn Hauptbahnhof (central station) is well connected to
Cologne and the wider region, with direct ICE connections to major German
cities.

**By air:** Cologne Bonn Airport (CGN) is roughly 30 minutes away.
Düsseldorf (DUS) and Frankfurt (FRA) airports offer wider international
connections with direct train links to Bonn.
