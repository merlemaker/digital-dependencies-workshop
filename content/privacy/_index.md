## Overview

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. This privacy policy
explains what data we collect when you visit this website and how it is used.
The responsible party within the meaning of data protection law is named in the
[Imprint](/imprint/).

## Hosting

This website is hosted by [name of hosting provider, e.g. GitHub Pages]. When
you visit the site, the provider automatically collects and stores information
in server log files that your browser transmits. This may include the IP
address, date and time of access, and the page requested. This data is used
solely to ensure the technical operation and security of the website and is not
merged with other data sources.

## Registration form

If you register for the workshop, we collect the data you enter in the
registration form (for example name, email address, institution, and any
catering preferences). This data is used exclusively to organise and run the
workshop and is not passed on to third parties without your consent.

## Contact by email

If you contact us by email, the information you provide will be stored for the
purpose of processing your enquiry and any follow-up questions. We do not pass
this data on without your consent.

## Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to
information about your stored data, and to its correction, deletion, or
restriction of processing. You also have the right to object to processing and
the right to data portability. To exercise these rights, or if you have
questions about data protection, please contact the responsible party named in
the [Imprint](/imprint/).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection
supervisory authority.
